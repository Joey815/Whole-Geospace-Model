# WACCM-X 2024-10-03 X9 Electron Density At Fixed Altitudes

Each frame contains flare, no-flare, and log10(flare/no-flare) electron density maps at 30, 60, 90, 150, and 250 km.
Only 10:00-14:00 UT is plotted.

- Electron density: `Ne = e * p / (k_B T)`, converted to `cm^-3`, then shown as `log10(Ne)`
- Height method: h1 `e` and `T` converted on model levels, then interpolated to fixed geometric altitudes using h2 `Z3` linearly interpolated in time
- Absolute maps use `jet`; log-ratio maps use `RdBu_r` centered at zero
- Colorbars are placed in independent right-side columns
- Overlays: Natural Earth 110m coastline, dashed solar terminator
- Cadence: 5 minutes
- Figure style: local clone of `Yuan1z0825/nature-skills`, `skills/nature-figure` Python/matplotlib rules
- Frames: 49
- 30 km log10(Ne cm^-3) color range: `-17.5881` to `-17.3493`; log-ratio range: `+-0.01` dex
- 60 km log10(Ne cm^-3) color range: `0.4142` to `2.15702`; log-ratio range: `+-0.341885` dex
- 90 km log10(Ne cm^-3) color range: `2.52179` to `5.08764`; log-ratio range: `+-1.03922` dex
- 150 km log10(Ne cm^-3) color range: `2.95935` to `5.61277`; log-ratio range: `+-0.269903` dex
- 250 km log10(Ne cm^-3) color range: `2.97477` to `6.79376`; log-ratio range: `+-0.437621` dex
