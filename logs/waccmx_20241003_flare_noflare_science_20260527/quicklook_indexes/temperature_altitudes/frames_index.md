# WACCM-X 2024-10-03 X9 Temperature At Fixed Altitudes

Each frame contains flare, no-flare, and flare minus no-flare neutral temperature maps at 60, 90, 150, and 250 km.
The top panel shows the FISM2 23-band SSI sum; the vertical line marks the frame time.

- Temperature variable: `T`
- Height method: h1 `T` interpolated vertically to fixed geometric altitudes using h2 `Z3` linearly interpolated between 2024-10-03 00:00 and 2024-10-04 00:00
- Overlays: Natural Earth 110m coastline, dashed solar terminator
- Cadence: 5 minutes
- Figure style: local clone of `Yuan1z0825/nature-skills`, `skills/nature-figure` Python/matplotlib rules
- Frames: 288
- 60 km T color range: `220.374` to `284.978` K; delta range: `+-0.04` K
- 90 km T color range: `169.702` to `226.633` K; delta range: `+-0.2` K
- 150 km T color range: `597.14` to `833.801` K; delta range: `+-2.95739` K
- 250 km T color range: `886.365` to `1439.61` K; delta range: `+-7.79583` K
